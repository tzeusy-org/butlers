# v1 Scope

This document is the scope boundary. If a capability is not named here, it
waits. Scope debates end here; descriptions and status do not live here.

## What v1 Ships

One deployment running every agent in `roster/` as a persistent daemon, with
intelligence confined to ephemeral goal invocations: one lead per goal and
optional depth-1 children, launched and brokered by the Switchboard control
plane (see [architecture.md](architecture.md) and
[RFC 0038](../legends-and-lore/rfcs/0038-goal-runtime-and-capability-authorization.md)).
v1 ships on this goal runtime after its hard cutover (owner decision,
2026-10-14). The scope registry is the nine catalog sections below;
descriptions and status live in
[lay-and-land/components.md](../lay-and-land/components.md) and in each
capability's spec under `openspec/specs/`.

### Core Infrastructure

- **Multi-butler daemon** --- every agent in `roster/` runs as a persistent
  daemon in one deployment.
- **Goal runtime** --- one lead per goal and optional depth-1 children,
  launched and brokered by the Switchboard control plane (RFC 0038).
- **Module system** --- pluggable capability units that only add tools.
- **Task scheduler** --- cron-driven scheduling with deterministic and
  LLM-dispatched modes.
- **LLM CLI spawner** --- ephemeral sessions with locked-down MCP configs,
  concurrency limits, and model routing.
- **Session logging** --- append-only record of every invocation.
- **State store** --- per-butler key-value store backed by PostgreSQL JSONB.

### Staffers

Infrastructure specialists, never owners of a life domain and excluded from
briefings.

- **Switchboard** --- ingress and goal control plane.
- **Messenger** --- outbound delivery.
- **QA** --- error detection and investigation.
- **Concierge** --- read-only system-plane answers (RFC 0030).

### Butlers

Domain specialists, one life domain each, bound by a manifesto.

- **General** --- freeform storage and catch-all assistance; holds owner
  projects, which are owner-authored, time-bounded charters that group goals,
  obligations and links and grant no authority.
- **Health** --- measurements, medications, conditions, symptoms, nutrition.
- **Relationship** --- contacts, interactions, life events, relationship
  context.
- **Finance** --- financial signal tracking and awareness.
- **Education** --- learning tracking and knowledge management.
- **Travel** --- trip planning and travel context.
- **Home** --- home automation awareness and environmental context.
- **Lifestyle** --- taste, music, entertainment, food, hobbies, routines.
- **Chronicler** --- retrospective reconstruction from other butlers'
  evidence; it does not plan, ingest, or notify beyond the daily day-close and
  its opted-in once-daily gap interview correction prompt, and distills only
  into a private schema it owns (RFC 0014).

### Modules

Metrics and Document renderer were retired from v1 by owner decision on
2026-10-04.

- **Memory** --- episodes, entity-anchored facts, and rules.
- **Contacts** --- tools over the shared contacts registry.
- **Calendar** --- Google Calendar event management.
- **Email** --- email-aware butlers.
- **Telegram** --- interactive Telegram messaging.
- **Approvals** --- safety gates for sensitive tool calls.
- **Pipeline** --- multi-step processing workflows.
- **Self-healing** --- error relay to the QA staffer (RFC 0015).
- **Home Assistant** --- smart home control and awareness.
- **Google Drive** --- file read, write, search, and organize.
- **Google Health** --- wellness data access.
- **WhatsApp** --- output-only send and reply tools.
- **Spotify** --- listening awareness; named by owner decision on 2026-10-20.
- **Steam** --- game library, playtime, and online-status awareness.
- **Insight broker** --- Switchboard-side proactive insight delivery.
- **Dashboard read** --- Concierge's read-only dashboard tools.

### Connectors

This list is the fixed v1 connector set that RFC 0018 anchors to; a connector
not named here is deferred, and the deferral rationale lives in that RFC and
the ideas ledger.

- **Telegram bot** --- Telegram Bot API adapter.
- **Telegram user client** --- user-account message adapter.
- **Gmail** --- Gmail ingestion adapter.
- **Google Calendar** --- calendar change-event adapter.
- **Google Drive connector** --- file metadata event adapter.
- **Google Health connector** --- wellness data polling adapter.
- **Home Assistant connector** --- smart home event adapter.
- **OwnTracks** --- location and waypoint webhook receiver.
- **Spotify connector** --- playback state polling adapter.
- **Steam connector** --- Steam Web API polling adapter.
- **ActivityWatch** --- activity event adapter.
- **WhatsApp user client** --- passive inbox ingestion.
- **Live listener** --- real-time audio transcription.
- **Heartbeat** --- periodic health check.
- **Context feeds** --- deterministic scheduled pulls owned by one butler, not
  `ingest.v1` connectors, admitted by owner decision on 2026-10-08: Atmosphere
  (Open-Meteo weather, air quality and pollen, Home), Flight status
  (AviationStack status for booked legs, Travel), SimpleFIN bank feed
  (balances and transactions, Finance). A context feed not named here is
  deferred like a connector, and a GitHub activity feed stays deferred.

### Dashboard

- **Operator console** --- the RFC 0038 D19 owner surface: goals with lineage,
  effects, approvals and Stop; domain regions; contacts and identity;
  ingestion monitoring. Its role and boundaries are defined in
  `docs/frontend/purpose-and-single-pane.md`.
- **Settings console** --- health, model catalog, spend, and owner-confirmed
  data export and wipe.
- **Audit log** --- append-only record of every configuration mutation.
- **Webhooks** --- signed outbound delivery with retry.
- **Web push** --- an installed dashboard may send content-free push: the
  payload is an opaque id only, and the lock screen shows a category only
  (owner decision, 2026-10-20).
- **Public read surface** --- one deterministic MCP server through which the
  owner's ChatGPT (including Dots) and claude.ai clients read Butlers data, as
  RFC 0039 defines (owner decision, 2026-11-04): typed read tools over a
  replicated projection, finance and health included, on one server with
  per-domain kill switches; the ChatGPT `search` and `fetch` contract; and
  `propose_fact`, which applies nothing until the owner approves each
  proposal. Other clients, such as Excel, wait.
- **Shares** --- owner decision, 2026-10-20: Butlers may create an
  approval-gated Google Drive share to a named person, and revoke it. It never
  creates an anyone-with-link share.
- **Email as the owner** --- owner decision, 2026-10-20: brand-new emails stay
  drafts the owner sends. Butlers sends as the owner only in-thread Gmail
  replies, each approved per event or under a standing rule the owner created
  where RFC 0038 permits one, plus Google's own share notice for an approved
  named-person share.
- **Messaging as the owner** --- owner decision, 2026-10-20: outbound messages
  never use the owner's personal WhatsApp or Telegram user-client accounts;
  Butlers drafts them for the owner to send.

### Identity System

- **Shared contacts registry** --- canonical entity registry with contact
  predicates.
- **Cross-channel identity resolution** --- mapping channel identifiers to
  canonical entities via contact predicates.
- **Owner bootstrapping** --- automatic owner entity creation on first startup.

### Situational Awareness

- **Context bus** --- shared TTL-bounded context signals readable by all
  butlers, pull-based and opt-in (RFC 0009).
- **Proactive insight delivery** --- butler-side generation, Switchboard-side
  brokering, and digest delivery via Messenger (RFC 0011).

### Observability

- **OpenTelemetry instrumentation** --- traces and metrics from the daemon,
  spawner, and modules.
- **OpenTelemetry Collector** --- telemetry collection and routing.

## What v1 Defers

Explicitly out of scope. No v1 work is designed to "prepare for" these at the
cost of v1 simplicity.

- **Discord.** Deferred by owner decision on 2026-10-04; the draft connector
  is retired. A return goes through a new proposal (RFC 0018, ideas ledger).
- **Multi-user support.** One user, one deployment. No accounts, no tenants.
- **Mobile application.** The dashboard is web-only. No native iOS or Android
  client.
- **Coding on the owner's repositories.** It belongs to the owner's own
  engineering tooling outside Butlers. Butlers has no engineering executor
  (owner decision, 2026-10-20).
- **Read-only page rendering.** Rendering pages a plain fetch cannot read is
  deferred until the research host measures how often page opens fail
  (owner decision, 2026-10-20).
- **Voice as an interface.** No speech-to-text or text-to-speech at the butler
  level. Connector/dashboard ingress may transcribe the verified owner's own
  non-forwarded Telegram voice note, Telegram audio file or dashboard recording
  through instruction-free ASR before admitting text under RFC 0038 D5/D18
  (modality decision `bu-2utmy5`, 2026-10-20). Authorship comes from bound ingress
  evidence, not transcript words; forwarded, quoted, room and non-owner material
  stays third-party. Finite length and execution bounds remain engineering
  configuration. This does not authorize an outside speech service, household
  or room voice, duplex/realtime voice, deployment, or a butler-level speech
  capability. Existing live-listener transport is not an owner-audio exception;
  in-room voice egress remains a gated proposal. Spoken replies/TTS are a
  separate contract, not part of this audio-authorship amendment.
- **Managed setup.** The Helm chart and compose files are deployment
  artifacts, not an installer. No setup wizard, no one-click deploy.
- **Plugin marketplace.** No third-party module distribution or plugin API
  versioning.
- **Automatic approval of outside proposals.** Every `propose_fact` proposal
  waits for the owner's per-event approval. Any automatic approval, by a model
  or a rule, needs its own owner decision (RFC 0039 D13).
- **Offline reasoning.** State persists locally; reasoning needs LLM access.
- **End-to-end encryption.** The owner owns the database; see
  [security.md](security.md).
- **Multi-LLM orchestration within one invocation.** Each lead or child
  invocation uses one provider; separate invocations of a goal may differ.

## Platform Targets

Linux (primary) and macOS (development); Python 3.12+; PostgreSQL 15+ with
JSONB; Docker Compose or k3s via the in-repo Helm chart; Claude Code as the
primary LLM runtime with model routing to Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode.

## Success Criteria

v1 is complete when:

1. Every staffer and domain butler in `roster/` runs concurrently in one
   deployment and handles its declared responsibilities.
2. Owner requests from every active ingress connector are admitted as goals,
   and on the replay evaluation set the goal runtime meets or beats the
   recorded outcomes of the classify-and-route runtime it replaced, graded on
   whole-goal success and owner corrections (lifestyle-only ambient connectors
   such as Steam are excluded from this set).
3. The adversarial authorization suite of RFC 0038 produces zero unauthorized
   effects.
4. Scheduled tasks fire on their cron cadence and retry transient failures.
5. The memory subsystem stores, retrieves, and consolidates facts across
   sessions.
6. The dashboard shows goals with their lineage, effects and approvals, each
   domain region's data and obligations, contacts, and ingestion flow in real
   time (RFC 0038 D19).
7. The system runs seven consecutive days with no manual intervention beyond
   LLM API key rotation.
8. The owner uses the system daily across at least three domains.
9. Proactive insights arrive at a sustainable cadence without manual tuning,
   and disengagement lowers the rank of ignored categories.

Evidence against these criteria lives in tests, benchmarks
(`tests/benchmarks/`), and Beads, never in this file; a dated status line here
goes stale the day it is written.

## Anti-Patterns

- Building v1 features with multi-user "hooks".
- Deferring reliability work because "it is just v1".
- Expanding connector coverage before existing connectors are solid.
- Adding modules no butler in the roster uses.
- Designing plugin abstractions before the internal module API is stable.
