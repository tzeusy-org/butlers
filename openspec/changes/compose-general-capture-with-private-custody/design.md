# General capture fold-in under RFC 0038

## Current authority and disposition

The complete `General Butler Tool Surface` requirement is folded into the
same-name block in
`../generalist-lead-focused-delegation/specs/butler-general/spec.md`.
Its ordinary capture scenario is carried verbatim; its collection/item/calendar
inventory is retained under the target's control-plane registration trigger.
The source no longer edits `butler-general`. Its six `general-capture`
requirements remain active and byte-identical. This is the emptied-moved-delta
disposition, not a whole-source archive.

RFC 0038 D1/D2/D5 replace capture classification and target-session dispatch with
deterministic admission of one goal per ingest source, reusing its goal on replay.
Admission uses server-held origin/cause, starting exposure and authority ceiling;
text, caller actor fields and old session locators confer no authority. A verified
owner capture must commit its domain-owned held receipt and return its opaque ID
before asynchronous reasoning or any target effect. Principal/source/payload
idempotency and changed-payload refusal remain independent of goal deduplication;
neither receipt proves the other or permits replay of an unknown target outcome.

General retains the capture ledger, receipts, collections, bounded search and
source-version/fence protocol. A goal invokes only registered, configured and
granted capabilities through the RFC 0038 D4/D7 broker and domain assertion
verification. Missing groups, source authority, privacy fencing or a compatible
target-owned receipt still refuse or hold before mutation. General-local target,
version and routed receipt commit atomically; specialist writes and exact outcome
reconciliation remain owning-domain MCP operations, never peer SQL or a generic
route dispatch. An opaque routed locator is no substitute for a verified receipt.

RFC 0037's private-parent exclusion, ordinary absent-name compatibility, durable
ambiguity, current-eligibility checks, source-owned catalog retirement and
commit-valid source/consumer fencing remain unchanged. D4/D15 keep private custody
unreachable by models; no grant can lift that boundary. Ordinary returned content
retains source/sensitivity stamps and exposure handling. Owner intake still checks
authentication/CSRF before body access and uses fixed no-body audit/error paths;
attachments remain source references, and UI states cannot claim Saved from a held
or unavailable outcome. No extra raw store, wire field, grant, model function or
custody capability is introduced.

`implementation-packets.json` preserves all nine original outcomes and complete
historical packet fields for comparison and outstanding-delivery reconciliation.
Its authority-disposition metadata supersedes the old classifier, generic-route,
current-session and spawner references, historical reservations and test estimates;
none is an executable successor instruction. Goal-runtime conversion remains
PENDING under `generalist-lead-focused-delegation`, with fresh source ownership,
real PostgreSQL/role/concurrency, intake/UI, privacy and exact-head CI evidence
still required before claiming delivery. The source's three checked tasks describe
only their recorded historical source/ordinary-boundary work; all five unfinished
tasks and all target task states remain unchanged. No implementation, deployment,
private operation, archive or RFC amendment is performed by this fold.
