## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Source admission and one hold authority

There SHALL be one authoritative custody-hold model. Any butler MAY initiate a bounded proposal via Switchboard MCP, but ordinary hold writes SHALL execute only on Switchboard-owned admitted service paths; no model-facing tool argument, DTO, origin name, source_entity_id or route endpoint string SHALL mint authority. Current owner-authenticated dashboard/host control SHALL be verified at its owning boundary and forwarded as a protected command, not an actor field. Enabled, owner-exempt, non-bypassable RLS SHALL exclude peer runtime hold mutation, release, DELETE and generation reset despite bootstrap public re-grants. A bounded content-blind read definer SHALL expose eligibility without granting peer private-schema access. Existing public-table accepted risks SHALL remain unchanged. Full supported independent-process custody admission SHALL remain mandatory: the cohosted Linux companion does not fulfil it. Every supported process SHALL use the complete proposed P-CUSTODY-1/P1 current-principal/source-owned protocol, including protected host enrollment, live DB backend incarnation, writer binding, canonical owning-source digest, destination/operation challenge and final current COMMITTING checks. These exact bounded P1c feature interfaces/EXEC remain proposed engineering under existing trusted startup authority, never installed facts or a generic missing-code owner gate. A cohost-only topology reduction is unselected and cannot count as completion. P1c SHALL use the complete constructor-fixed source-owning adapter and actual dedicated backend/startup enrollment under the existing trusted host/daemon/SET ROLE/MCP premise. No external caller or ordinary same-role unbound backend SHALL choose its source family, bind an arbitrary nonce or self-enroll. Live DB backend_start/incarnation, actual writer acquisition generation and explicit UNBIND, canonical own-row digest, destination/operation online challenge, registered guard and final current owner/control/target checks SHALL be mandatory across all supported topologies. P1c SHALL NOT claim hostile same-UID parent-memory/filesystem/FD, compromised trusted connecting-admin-login or hostile-host isolation; current permission-bypass/env/mode0400 supplies none. This limitation SHALL NOT waive any original effective-role/MCP/owner-auth/hold-generation/provider/recovery behavior.

### Requirement: Callback and approved execution fences

The current core_233 owner definer and all owner-only consumers SHALL exclude active holds without weakening malformed/inactive/merged/deleted/collision or channel-normalization rules. Telegram approval callbacks from a held identity SHALL be acknowledgement-only with no approve/reject mutation. Already queued/approved execution SHALL recheck source and egress eligibility at its actual executor/provider admission boundary; a standing rule or older approval cannot bypass an active custody hold. Current primary-only callback policy and uniform primary/secondary outbound bypass remain otherwise unchanged. A provider-start operation admitted before the hold commit SHALL retain its actual confirmed/ambiguous result; the hold cannot retroactively undo it or assert no send.

### Requirement: Bounded target identity and scope

A custody hold SHALL identify a current exact owner-associated target or the closed complete versioned set of ALL known owner-associated bindings of the proven provider for the original unresolved-affected-account fallback. Canonicalization SHALL reuse the current typed ambiguity-safe identity candidate universe. A receiving bot/mailbox, provider label, account credential scope and OwnTracks device SHALL NOT be interchangeable. Exact affected account unknown SHALL NOT cause a guessed account/device hold: the admitted owner NO SHALL atomically hold the actual complete known provider set under P1 inventory/version locks, or record truthful containment unknown if provider/cohort/completeness is unavailable. Complete empty inventory is explicitly no known target, never all-held success. An owner MAY choose an exact versioned multi-target set. This preserves original A2 fail-wide intent within the actual known owner-associated provider boundary; no new permission or unrelated-provider scope is implied.

### Requirement: Cheap owner-resolved lock and expensive release

A hold SHALL require an explicit owner act admitted through current protected dashboard/host authority, a current event/target-bound protected security callback, OR the P1 closed endpoint_lock branch from any canonical currently owner-resolved surviving channel INCLUDING unauthenticated email. The cheap branch SHALL authorize only LOCK of its exact owner-associated selection and SHALL NOT grant authenticated owner status, release/replaced, steering, approvals, session revocation or credential authority. Provider alerts without owner acts, generic confirm, arbitrary caller DTOs, missing/ambiguous/non-owner/held-source resolution and LLM inference SHALL NOT commit a hold. Canonical accepted-source proof and current source/control/target/version/clock checks repeat under SAME committing transaction locks. Spoofable owner-resolved email may cause containment denial of service and existing owner-default notice fallback/parking; those limits SHALL be documented honestly rather than treating the source as authenticated. Release SHALL require a current owner-authenticated dashboard session or explicit trusted host act, never held endpoint/email/yes/case close/timer/LLM. Optional revoke-all-dashboard-sessions remains an explicit protected owner/host act. This preserves original A1; the earlier protected-only proposal assertion is explicitly superseded.

### Requirement: Linearizable episode and truthful failure

Hold, active-episode uniqueness, generation advancement, required control receipt and canonical case correlation SHALL commit atomically before acknowledged success. Repeated commands return the existing admitted episode; concurrent hold/release/replace commands serialize by canonical target and compare expected hold/generation. A stale release cannot clear a successor hold. A failed transaction/cancellation before commit leaves no acknowledged partial change. Lost commit acknowledgement is unknown and resolved from the same command identity, not retried as a new act. Lookup/storage unavailability SHALL deny owner bypass, privileged execution and held-account egress without inventing a healthy result; generic identity may still be a report. Optional evidence outages SHALL NOT undo containment.
