## Ownership and proposed wire contract

Travel owns `trip_dependency_links` and `trip_impact_revisions` as minimal domain projections. No Calendar/Relationship rows, provider booking bodies or generic owner-data store are copied. All cross-domain reads use broker capabilities with original labels and source authority. The consumer cannot write source itinerary, Calendar or contact rows.

An owner-authenticated link command supplies `trip_id`, `leg_id`, `target_kind=travel_reservation|calendar_occurrence`, an opaque target reference, target source version, and an explicit arrival relation: `latest_arrival_at` (offset-aware UTC instant) and `transfer_minutes` (integer 0..360, owner-confirmed, never inferred). Calendar references bind provider/account/calendar/event plus occurrence start, not a recurring series or title. Reservation references bind the canonical Travel reservation. Creation returns stable `link_id`, revision and receipt; edits/removal require expected revision. A link grants neither a new read scope nor effect permission. Unreadable targets refuse enrollment; later revocation makes their result unknown and suppresses target detail. Maximum 32 active links per trip, 8 monitored legs across the feature and 8 concurrently enrolled trips. The UI rejects excess explicitly rather than silently dropping plans.

The authoritative Travel writer commits an accepted itinerary revision and an outbox reference atomically. Its envelope is `travel.itinerary_revision.v1` with opaque `trip_id`, `leg_id`, immutable source event key, monotonic trip-wide accepted revision, accepted_at, source_observed_at nullable, fetched_at nullable, timestamp_basis enum, arrival_basis enum (`provider_reported|owner_confirmed|departure_shift|missing`), current canonical source reference and joined labels. Cancellation/diversion is a typed canonical status, never parsed in the model. Every accepted change on any leg advances the same trip-wide revision under a serialized/CAS writer, so concurrent legs cannot reuse it; the revision identifies the resulting complete trip snapshot, not a leg-local counter. The event has no title, URL, recipient or arbitrary text. Same event key with changed digest refuses; replays reuse original times. The domain writer alone resolves competing/out-of-order sources or marks the accepted view unknown; the lead cannot select its preferred estimate.

This is a proposed publisher contract, not an existing registered event. Activation requires registration under the existing event/catalog authority and a compatible adopted perception interface. If the S3 closed kind registry is used, its narrowly scoped proposed extension is: kind `travel.itinerary_revision`, subject canonical trip UUID, primitive opaque reference to the Travel accepted-revision record, category `travel`, writer the authenticated Travel domain revision producer, owner-authorized detail visibility, freshness resolved from that record and the admitted profile. Its typed reference conveys no raw booking content. The extension needs explicit contract adoption; the consumer cannot self-register or bypass the closed registry.

An enrollment has a stable UUID and monotonic generation. A review has immutable key `(enrollment_id, enrollment_generation, trip_id, trip_revision, link_set_revision)`, monotonic generation, profile revision, original source/receipt/accepted times, per-link source versions and evidence references, goal reference if admitted, state, reason and canonical owner-delivery subject reference. Update a current review by CAS over generation and the current Travel/link-set revisions. A target read returns its own version; do not pretend cross-domain reads were one distributed snapshot. Before publishing results, revalidate the Travel/link-set revisions and target versions, or expose unknown/superseded. Target detail is bounded to authorized typed dates/status/references. No duplicated event, goal, approval or delivery ledger.

Report held, outage and miss cohorts separately while retaining the complete denominator. These are activation targets; test data plus a live canary must establish them. ## Deterministic assessment and lifecycle

For an offset-aware, fresh, independently reported or owner-confirmed arrival and a readable current link, compare arrival plus owner-confirmed transfer buffer with latest_arrival_at. Strictly later means affected; equal or earlier means unaffected **for that declared arrival relation only**. A current authoritative cancellation means affected; diversion without a verified arrival at the required destination means unknown. Unknown provider freshness, extrapolated arrival, unreadable target, unsupported recurrence, missing time zone or conflicting source means unknown, with a separately labelled projected conflict where useful. An absence of assessed links reads `not assessed`, not `all plans safe`. The model may explain evidence or formulate alternatives but cannot promote unknown to confirmed or add unlinked plans.

State transitions: `pending -> assessing -> ready|partial|inference_held|failed`; any current state can become `superseded|expired|stopped`. `partial` preserves per-link unknowns or failed reads; `inference_held` preserves the deterministic packet. `superseded`, `expired` and `stopped` revoke all uncommitted choices. A new revision may reuse the existing canonical owner thread/subject with a new generation; the old immutable review stays historical. Cancelled-then-recovered within a coalescing window remains in evidence, while the latest valid state governs the current card. Repeated source delivery or owner Review now against the same current composite revision reuses the existing result/admission; it cannot replenish the invocation budget. Superseding a running review fences its publication and requests cancellation through the canonical runtime. Its invocation continues to occupy its per-trip and feature concurrency slots until termination is confirmed, and its token reservation remains charged until authoritative settlement (unknown usage stays conservatively reserved). A replacement cannot reuse those occupied slots or refunded capacity merely because the review state changed. Only the newest unexpired candidate remains queued; stop/cancellation failures are visible rather than permission for another concurrent invocation.

An owner link edit creates a new link-set revision and invalidates the old review; a source-only duplicate does not. Removing one link invalidates its options immediately and re-evaluates remaining links. A changed target version makes the stored arrival relation unknown until the owner confirms it against that new version; do not silently move an owner-confirmed deadline. Stop fences publication, model admission and every subsequent effect dispatch; already committed effects remain recorded. Renewal can create a new current review only with its new enrollment generation and fresh source evidence, never by reopening an old review or relabelling historical evidence; all generations share the stable feature and per-trip concurrency/budget accounting. Stopping does not disable existing flight/connection alerts or pretend to cancel a booking.

## Later exact choices and authority

The initial profile exposes read-only review, Open source, Review now and Stop monitoring. It offers no active mutation or send controls. Future `approved_choices` requires adopted M7 decision sets and source-owner conditional writes. It may prepare a message draft for a named owner-selected recipient, or propose an exact update to one Calendar occurrence; preparing a draft never sends it. Commercial booking cancellation/rebooking/payment remains excluded.

Each proposed item binds review/generation, Travel and link-set revisions, exact target reference and target version, capability/argument/recipient digest, effect key and expiry. The canonical set UI displays each exact item and supports unticking. Approval is per selected exact item; neither a whole-trip intent nor a prior set approval widens it. Immediately before dispatch, revalidate source premises and target versions. A mismatched/stale premise refuses and requires a new explicit proposal; do not substitute current arguments. Conditional target mutation must enforce version at the owning service/provider write boundary, not only a prior read. Since today's Calendar update tool does not expose this precondition, future Calendar choice activation is blocked until its owner supplies and verifies that contract.

Effect completion comes only from canonical committed receipts. `failed`, `partial` and `unknown` stay distinct; ambiguous provider execution is not automatically replayed. Retry reuses its original key only when the owning effect contract proves safe and premises remain current. A rollback disables new admission and cancels pending choices; undoing a committed edit requires a new authorized action. No sender/recipient is derived from airline prose, and no link implies cross-domain permission.

## Retention and privacy

Proposed initial policy: active link/review data remains available until enrollment ends, then immutable minimized review receipts, source/target opaque refs and digests are retained for 90 days after terminal disposition. Link records are retained as tombstones for the same period after removal. No new raw-provider, mail, Calendar body or contact-content retention is introduced. Raw evidence resolves through its owning store and may expire earlier; missing evidence is reported unavailable. Audit/effect/delivery receipts retain their existing independent policies and are not deleted by this feature. After 90 days delete feature detail; retain a compact enrollment-generation tombstone and source acceptance watermark for 400 days to refuse replay. Inputs older than the admitted freshness/horizon are history-only even after dedupe detail expires, so deleting retention data cannot resurrect a review. Explicit owner deletion follows existing deletion authority and leaves only its permitted anti-replay/audit disposition.

Existing selected trip/leg pre-fills context, but transfer buffer/deadline are shown for confirmation.

No-data, no links, denied access, stale source and source outage have different empty/error states with a next action. Stop remains visible during loading, budget holds and errors. Detail may expand without hiding the result or requiring the user to read raw telemetry.

## Failure reporting boundary

Five-second miss detection assumes a live recorder or caller. If persistence/control-plane service is unavailable, the live API/UI caller surfaces timeout or unavailable within five seconds and never confirms the operation; durable reconciliation later preserves the original deadline and failure timestamps. A complete process/host outage is outside the supported latency conditions and is reported as an outage on recovery, never as timely success or proof of a durable write during downtime.
