## Why

The owner intent API already records choices, but the Decisions page exposes only read-only context and status. `bu-tku4df` needs an exact controls contract before its original choose, pending, repeat/conflict, degraded, accessible triage and actual-applier smoke outcomes can be implemented.

This is an unadopted source-only contract candidate (`bu-fqru5q`) at baseline `b747a55a530364d987fb37457c343193e9362fe7`. It publishes the complete proposed replacement payload in `design.md` and its integration contract, without altering effective capability specs or the foreign-owned merged bridge package. `skip_specs: true` describes this documentation-only deliverable; it does not declare the proposed product behavior irrelevant or implemented.

## What Changes

- Specify native labelled controls for explicit offered options through the existing owner intent API, with the exact server-set actor/source, current owner authentication and CSRF client, and durable intent/applier boundaries unchanged.
- Specify same-option `200`/`meta.created=false` and conflicting-option `409` as Already handled, captured-row concurrency, source-unavailable disabling, confirmed response separated from failed refresh, and unknown outcomes without automatic mutation replay.
- Carry full proposed same-name replacements for canonical **Read-Only Decision Detail and Selection** and merged active **Recorded Decision Intent State Is Visible**, plus complete offered-option scenarios. Preserve every unrelated current scenario body and retain headings, including the affected legacy read-only heading.
- Record the two necessary release gates: exact independently reviewed owner adoption of the full artifact, and separately allocated canonical integration of both prohibitions by the existing contract owners. A draft, green checks, source landing, or an inert new requirement cannot supply either gate.
- Keep implementation, operational smoke and all future integration/rollout work unchecked. Silence retains the current read-only page.
