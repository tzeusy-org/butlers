## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Events Outside the Owner's Declared Hours Are Flagged

ID: REQ-calendar-conflict-overcommitment-radar-007
Source: bu-5ii4gj.9 (declared-hours guard)
Scope: v1-mandatory

The radar SHALL report an `outside_declared_hours` issue for a timed event whose start, end or day
breaks the owner's declared scheduling preferences (earliest and latest meeting time, meeting days,
no-meeting blocks), judged in the preferences' own timezone by the same check the slot ranking uses.
Each issue SHALL cover one event and carry `reasons` naming the first limit broken, in the order
`off_day`, `before_earliest`, `after_latest`, `no_meeting_block`. Only an event the declared hours
apply to SHALL be judged: one the owner attends (accepted, tentative or not yet answered), opaque,
timed, not generated by a butler and not a declared status (`outOfOffice`, `workingLocation`,
`focusTime`). A declined, free, all-day or declared-status event SHALL NOT be flagged, and a
quarantined invitation SHALL have no flag and no entry. The severity SHALL be `warning` when the
owner has not answered the event or it repeats, and `info` otherwise. No title or description SHALL
be read. With no declared hours, or none that constrain, no issue SHALL be reported.

When the declared hours cannot be read the radar SHALL report no `outside_declared_hours` issue and
SHALL mark the scan's hours as unavailable; a producer that resolves conditions on a clean scan
SHALL NOT read the missing issues as "all within hours" and SHALL skip that tick.

The invitations inbox SHALL carry the issue on its entry like any other radar issue, and the
overcommitment producer SHALL qualify a `warning` `outside_declared_hours` issue as a title-free
condition keyed on the event's provider identity and the reason. The flag only informs: it SHALL NOT
answer, move or decline anything.

#### Scenario: An unanswered invitation before the earliest meeting time

- **WHEN** a not yet answered invitation from a trusted organizer starts before the declared earliest time
- **THEN** the radar SHALL report a warning `outside_declared_hours` issue with the reason `before_earliest`
- **AND** the invitations inbox SHALL show the flag on that entry

#### Scenario: An accepted one-off outside the hours is information

- **WHEN** an accepted event that does not repeat ends after the declared latest time
- **THEN** the issue SHALL be `info` with the reason `after_latest`

#### Scenario: Events the hours do not govern are not flagged

- **WHEN** the event is declined, free, all-day, out of office or focus time
- **THEN** no `outside_declared_hours` issue SHALL be reported

#### Scenario: Hours judged in the owner's zone

- **WHEN** the declared hours carry a timezone and an event is inside them in that zone but outside them in UTC
- **THEN** no issue SHALL be reported

#### Scenario: Unreadable hours are not a clean bill

- **WHEN** the declared hours cannot be read
- **THEN** no issue SHALL be reported, the scan's hours SHALL be marked unavailable and the overcommitment producer SHALL skip that tick
