## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-approvals authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-approvals requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: exact-call owner authorization, delivery versus decision truth, pending/stalled coverage, expiry, defer, abandonment, retry predicates, prepared origin and promotion-family separation. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. D19 adds goal/invocation/exposure/cause and cancelled/unknown refusal, preserving all old approval clauses. Direct authored recipient/content Submit has its own server approval and is excluded from promotion. Untrusted prepared effects stay parked. Routing promotion cannot change deterministic admission policy; retiring that routing UI requires its owner-controlled collision amendment. No old execution or recipient authority is inferred from the new labels.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
