## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-chat-ui authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-chat-ui requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: safe message rendering, accessible recall/search/composer, immutable retry identity, honest streaming, draft retention and in-thread errors. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. A single assistant goal/thread surface replaces the two unit chat postures. Public unit identity and current-unit switching retire; server-held invocation/region provenance remains inspectable. Sanitized partial rendering, safe citations and persistence are specified in dashboard-goal-message-rendering. The current chat remains until an allocated migration implements every case.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
