## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-visibility authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-visibility requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: historical session/trace evidence, redaction, null provenance, navigation, filtering, cost and request-trace reconciliation. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. New goal/invocation doors use stored foreign keys only. Old session IDs remain historical read-only evidence and are never guessed into goal identities. Session Explorer geometry can retire only with a complete goal/trace navigation migration.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
