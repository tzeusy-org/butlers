### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-education-api authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-education-api requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: validated receipt-before-work submission, one-open partial-index guard, status reads, full lifecycle, notification evidence, paired CHECK constraints and abandonment sweep. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. The future producer is an education obligation goal, with stable request and actual invocation correlation; it cannot fabricate legacy session IDs. The complete goal receipt contract retains every delivered/no_record/unproven and failure case. Current trigger/session producer remains until the education owner allocates its exact migration.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
