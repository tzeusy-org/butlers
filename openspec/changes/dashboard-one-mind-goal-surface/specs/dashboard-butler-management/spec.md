## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-butler-management authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-butler-management requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: actual domain records, schedules, source availability, configuration inspection, prompt provenance, memory facts, and unrelated memory/contact navigation. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. Region resident view owns domain records/obligations; Operator is read-only. Unit Sessions, Trigger, MCP debugging, routing log and delegation controls retire. Existing authenticated authoring APIs are not silently revoked or moved into Operator. System/Settings retains actual infrastructure controls; domain edits require the original service authority. Facts/Rules/Episodes and contact replace-navigation remain byte-for-byte.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
