## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-overview authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-overview requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: truthful whole-population triage, unavailable sources, action eligibility, caching, period/currency and safe drill-down. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. Completed goals today is the proposed successor hero, counted only from D11 completion and actual owner-reply delivery. Active goals, due obligations and decisions replace unit/session editorial hierarchy. Unknown/partial/failed/stopped/budget-exhausted receipts remain supporting facts; old runtime facts move to System only where their actual source exists.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
