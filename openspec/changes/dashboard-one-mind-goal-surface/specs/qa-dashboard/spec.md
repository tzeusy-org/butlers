### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves qa-dashboard authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing qa-dashboard requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: case diagnosis, watch/patrol evidence, breaker tri-state, source failures, safe case/PR doors and authentic control receipts. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. QA case/dossier remains a System/domain workspace. Session doors become stored invocation/goal doors when the QA executor actually supplies them. D19 grants no QA executor category, retry, circuit-breaker reset or provider route.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
