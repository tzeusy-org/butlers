## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-conversations authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-conversations requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: immutable message/thread identity, owner-authenticated cancellation and recovery, persistence, search, stream observation, safe citations and honest terminal receipts. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. The new input is admitted deterministically into a goal or its actual named terminal action. Classifier route lanes and provider resume execution retire at M6 under D3/D9; durable anchors, cancellation, reply idempotency and historical data remain. The full pre-admission and citation contracts below govern the successor; active terminal-recovery and continuity owners retain integration authority.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
