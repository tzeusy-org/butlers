## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: D19 dark surface preserves dashboard-model-settings authority

The specification-only D19 change SHALL leave every existing dashboard-model-settings requirement and scenario normative and unchanged at its existing functional boundary: global catalog/configuration truth and historical model, tier, override and failure evidence. It SHALL NOT remove, replace, reinterpret or activate those contracts by adding a dark goal/region interface specification. Unit trigger complexity and per-unit runtime override controls cannot configure an invocation after D3/D7. Future goal detail displays actual selected model/tier/role from the invocation receipt. Global settings survive independently; removing legacy override APIs/rows is a separate exact model-owner amendment, not a dashboard permission.
Any M6 integration SHALL have an allocated complete clause-by-clause amendment and fresh independent semantic review with the actual current capability and active-change owner. A historical body annex, matching scenario name, unchanged count, separate path, green source guard or final owner silence SHALL NOT establish that a future producer or consumer preserves a guarantee.
