Custody barrier: revoke epoch -> refuse all new uses/publication/copy results -> cancel/fence active users -> dispose/retire every full holder -> acknowledge custody completion. Crash/lost ack/unknown provider disposal stays revoked/unavailable; never report complete or restore old text. Existing finite deadlines bound attempts; private revocation never grants another paid turn or waives Stop/ambiguity. No client can dereference even a valid-looking private ID. Raw source deletion/retention and unrelated records remain under their own owner.

A protected overlay may aid finding earlier context, but ordinary visible answers/actions still require authorized raw-source admission and normal domain/tool/risk/approval/audit. No information-flow safety is inferred from a model's claim that its output is safe. Every holder must have a trusted source/projection and executing gate before this feature runs. No implementation or current custody proof is claimed by this table.
