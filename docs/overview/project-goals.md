# Project Goals

> **Purpose:** Connect the product's purpose to its governing principles and scope.
> **Audience:** Contributors and evaluators understanding the project's direction.
> **Prerequisites:** [What Is Butlers?](what-is-butlers.md)

Butlers exists to reduce the recurring mental work of daily life while keeping
its owner informed and in control. Domain specialists retain context and act
through their tools, schedules, and the Switchboard. The daemon supplies
deterministic infrastructure; reasoning belongs in ephemeral LLM sessions.

The [vision](../../about/heart-and-soul/vision.md) owns the full thesis,
non-negotiable rules, and anti-patterns. Use it to evaluate whether a proposal
belongs in Butlers. The [architecture doctrine](../../about/heart-and-soul/architecture.md)
explains the structural choices, and the [security doctrine](../../about/heart-and-soul/security.md)
defines trust and approval boundaries.

The [v1 scope](../../about/heart-and-soul/v1.md) separates current commitments
from deferred ideas. The [v1 status](../../about/heart-and-soul/v1-status.md)
records evidence against those commitments. This page deliberately carries no
second feature inventory or production-readiness claim.
