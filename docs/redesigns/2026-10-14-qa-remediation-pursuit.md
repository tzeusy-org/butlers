1. **A: QA-owned execution and launch lineage.** A core-owned, lifecycle-fenced launch capability carries QA prompt/configuration, model choice, session store and accounting. Both initial and follow-up runs leave a durable launch receipt before execution. Restores vision Rules 2, 5–6 and the existing QA concurrency contract. Highest priority because all subsequent evidence depends on who actually ran.
2. **B: Fresh evidence and truthful outcomes.** Replace tracked notes with invocation-bound output; preserve history with contamination/uncertainty annotations. Separate inconclusive, blocked, operational, already-fixed, design-required and patch-ready diagnoses from execution/publication/recovery. Update existing dossier consumers and full-proposal diff evidence together.
3. **C: Durable incident admission.** Persist pending work, trusted source linkage, leases and atomic acknowledgement. Meaningful evidence changes qualify new reasoning; cooldown alone does not. Three launched sessions/sixty active minutes per incident generation is the proposed default, shared with feedback follow-ups. Preserve publisher ambiguity holds and all ordinary admission protections.
4. **D: Bounded discovery with real coverage.** Durable source progress, overlap, fair continuation and catch-up replace lookback-as-coverage. Partial/error/retention gaps remain visible. Consume run18's log-source replacement rather than designing a competing backend. Observation and reasoning stay separate.
5. **E: Governed proposals and exact feedback.** Investigators ground fixes in governing contracts and proportionate verification. Boundary changes become proposals for review. Complete PR/head/check/inline-thread evidence drives one bounded follow-up; existing restricted publisher retains sole remote mutation authority and ambiguous-operation holds.
6. **F: Recovery and usage with evidence.** Match deployed revision to a safe regression check or complete relevant recurrence opportunity. Empty patrols cannot prove repair. Expose an incident/attempt/PR-qualified funnel and linked usage coverage; retain unknown billing and served identity. Keep existing Dispatch surfaces and evidence doors.
7. **G: One cross-child reconciliation/report.** Falsify coverage across every affected path, reuse executing proof, publish an epic report and refresh mandate coverage. Uncovered mandates return to milestone synthesis; unrelated broad readiness work keeps its existing owner.
